Privacy Policy

Effective 14 October 2026

In short
  • Verb Hop collects no personal information. Nothing about your child leaves the device.
  • Children's names and results are kept only on the device, and parents can delete them at any time.
  • Voice recordings are checked on the device, held for a few seconds, and never saved or sent.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no accounts.

About this policy

This policy explains how the Verb Hop app ("Verb Hop", "we", "us") handles information. Verb Hop is an app for children aged 5 to 8 and their parents or teachers. It teaches English verb forms with pictures, spoken sentences and a game in which children say the sentences aloud.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information, from children or from adults. The app has no sign-in, sends no data to us or to anyone else, and contains no advertising, analytics or tracking software.

Information kept on the device

So that the app can work, it keeps the following on the device itself, in the app's own storage:

  • Each child's first name, if a parent enters one (it is optional), and the picture chosen for them.
  • Game results: which sentences were picked, how many tries they took, whether a sentence was said right, and when each game was played.
  • Settings, such as the language of the instructions and whether the app checks what the child says.

This information is never sent off the device. It is used only to show the child's progress in the app and in the Parents area.

The microphone

When a child taps "Say the sentence", the app records the child saying the sentence, plays the recording back, and uses speech recognition built into the app to check whether the sentence was right. All of this happens on the device. The recording is held in the device's memory for a few seconds, until the playback and the check are done, and is then deleted. Recordings are never saved, never sent anywhere, and never used for any other purpose.

The app asks for permission before it uses the microphone. If permission is refused, the app still works; only the speaking parts are switched off. Parents can also turn the speech check off in the Parents area.

Purchases

Verb Hop can be tried for free and unlocked with a one-time purchase. Purchases are made and processed by Google Play under Google's own terms and privacy policy. We never see payment details; the app only learns whether the unlock has been bought.

Permissions the app uses

  • Microphone, for the speaking game, as described above.
  • Internet, only so that Google Play can complete and restore the purchase. The app needs no internet connection to play.

Children's privacy

Verb Hop is designed for children. Because it collects no personal information, it does not collect, use or disclose personal information from children, as defined by the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) in the United States and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) in the European Union. A parent or guardian sets the app up in the Parents area, which is protected by a simple question that young children cannot answer.

Deleting information

Everything Verb Hop keeps is on the device and is under the control of whoever uses it. In the Parents area you can delete a single session, delete all of a child's results, or remove a child. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page with a new effective date. If a change ever meant that information would leave the device, we would explain it in the app before it took effect.
