Yesterday, now or tomorrow?

Verb Hop helps children aged 5 to 8 hear and use the right verb form. Two rabbits, Bop and Béa, act out 42 everyday verbs. Children pick the sentence that fits the picture, then say it aloud.

How it works

Listen and pick

Every question and sentence is spoken, so children who cannot read yet can play.

Say it aloud

Children say the sentence and hear themselves back. The app checks it on the device and cheers when it is right.

Gentle by design

Hints, never "wrong!". Common mistakes like "runned" and "eated" are taught, not punished.

For parents

Progress for each child, no ads, no accounts, and nothing ever leaves the device.
